# Product Guidelines: Votify - Plataforma Inteligente de Votación

## Vision & Tone
Votify's communication and design should embody a professional yet engaging tone, complemented by an informative and approachable style. This ensures that users feel confident in the platform's capabilities while also finding it easy to understand and interact with.

## Branding Elements
The platform will convey a modern and minimalist aesthetic. This approach will be reflected in clean design, efficient use of space, and a focus on essential elements, contributing to a sophisticated and contemporary user experience.

## UI/UX Principles
The following UI/UX principles are paramount:
-   **Simplicity & Intuition:** Prioritize ease of use and intuitive navigation. The design should guide users effortlessly through their tasks, minimizing learning curves.
-   **Consistency & Predictability:** Maintain consistent design patterns, interactions, and user flows across the entire platform. Users should be able to predict how elements will behave, fostering a sense of familiarity and control.
-   **Feedback & Error Prevention:** Provide clear, timely feedback for user actions and proactively design to prevent errors. When errors do occur, guide users effectively to resolution.

### Visual Identity (from GEMINI.md)
-   **Roles:** Org: `#2563EB`, Part: `#8B5CF6`, Jur: `#F97316`, Pub: `#10B981`.
-   **UI:** Cards with `rounded-[32px]` corners, Glassmorphism effects where appropriate, and typography using Poppins/Inter fonts.

## Accessibility Considerations
-   **Keyboard Navigation:** Ensure the platform is fully navigable using only a keyboard, supporting users who rely on keyboard-only interaction.
